Cookies & browsing memory

Last updated October 5, 2026

What this website uses

Our website code does not set HTTP cookies. It uses browser storage for your privacy choice and, only when you allow it, optional navigation features. No Meta Pixel, advertising tracker, analytics script or third-party embedded content is installed. Hosting providers may process technical request logs as described in our Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Optional browsing memory is off until you choose “Allow browsing memory.” “Reject optional storage” leaves the catalog, filters, product pages, image viewer and contact links usable, but saved return-position memory and shared-image handoffs will not be available. You can change your choice at any time.

Storage details

  • Privacy choice: local storage key avanti-privacy-choice-v1 stores your Allow/Reject choice and an expiry time for up to 180 days. It is used to respect your decision, not for tracking.
  • Shopping position: with permission, session storage keys avanti-catalog-position and avanti-catalog-return store the catalog URL, including filters/search, and scroll position during that tab session.
  • Image transition: with permission, avanti-photo-navigation stores a selected public image path, destination URL and time. It is consumed on arrival, with a maximum usable age of 15 seconds.

These values are not sent to Avanti Jewels. Rejecting optional storage clears these navigation entries. Browsers may restore a tab session when reopening tabs. You can clear site data in browser settings; if storage is blocked, the website remains usable but may ask again about preferences.

External links and future changes

Email, telephone, WhatsApp, social media and Venmo services have their own practices after you open them. We do not load their advertising or embedded content in this website. If analytics, ads, forms or embeds are added later, this policy and the preference controls must be reviewed before they are enabled.
