# Avanti Jewels client content checklist

- Sold-out product numbers are maintained in `products.js` and visibly tested.
- Current client-supplied prices are maintained in `products.js` and visibly tested.

- Materials, dimensions, finish, and included components when available.

- Business hours: Monday-Friday, 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Eastern Time.
- Payment methods: cash and Venmo.
- Reservations: by phone for 24 hours with customer contact information.
- All sales are final: no returns or exchanges.
- Local pickup and local delivery are available by arrangement.
- No shipping, alterations, deposits, installment plans or custom orders.
- Pickup address and delivery details are provided by inquiry.

## Business details

- Legal business name: Avanti Jewels.
- Preferred public phone number, email address, and WhatsApp number.
- Final Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok links.

- Revised theme and wording approved by the client.
