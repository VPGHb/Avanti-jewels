# Avanti Jewels legal launch checklist

The public Terms of Service and Privacy Policy currently match the site's catalog, direct-inquiry, and local-pickup workflow. Before the client approves publication, confirm the items below and update the policies where necessary.

## Confirmed commercial terms

- Business name: Avanti Jewels.
- Business hours: Monday-Friday, 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Eastern Time.
- Payment methods: cash and Venmo.
- Reservations: by phone for 24 hours with customer contact information; no deposit.
- All sales are final, with no returns or exchanges except rights that cannot legally be waived.
- Local pickup and local delivery are available by arrangement.
- No shipping, alterations, deposits, installment plans or custom orders.

## Launch handling

- Current prices and sold-out product numbers are client-supplied in `products.js`.
- Applicable taxes and charges are disclosed directly before payment.
- Local delivery area, timing, charges and delay options are confirmed directly before purchase.
- Pickup and delivery details are supplied by inquiry; no public address was supplied for launch.
- The Privacy Policy states that the general-audience website is not directed to children under 13.

## Update the Privacy Policy before enabling

- Analytics or session-recording tools.
- Advertising pixels, retargeting, or cross-site tracking.
- Newsletter or SMS marketing.
- Customer accounts, wish lists, or saved carts.
- Online checkout or a payment processor.
- Appointment scheduling or customer relationship management software.
- A cookie-consent or privacy-choice system.

## Professional review

Have a qualified New York attorney review the approved commercial terms and privacy disclosures before online payment, shipping, subscriptions, or targeted advertising are launched. Re-review the policies whenever the site's data practices or sales workflow materially changes.
