1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Avanti Jewels handles information when you browse avantijewels.com or contact us about a product. It applies to the current website and direct inquiries. Third-party websites and services linked from our site have their own privacy practices.

2. Information we handle

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, telephone, WhatsApp or social media, we may receive your name, contact details, message, product interests, preferred contact method and information you choose to include. A phone reservation requires your name and contact information. If you arrange a purchase, pickup or local delivery, we may also handle transaction, Venmo payment-confirmation, recipient and fulfillment details needed to complete it.

Website and device information

The current website does not create customer accounts or process payments. Our hosting and network providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Third-party interactions

If you use Venmo or follow a link to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp, your email provider or your telephone service, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

  • respond to questions and product inquiries;
  • create and manage 24-hour phone reservations;
  • confirm availability, purchases, local pickup and local delivery;
  • confirm cash or Venmo payment;
  • provide requested photographs, measurements or customer support;
  • maintain transaction and business records;
  • protect the website, customers and Avanti Jewels from fraud, misuse and security threats;
  • comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes; and
  • improve our products, service and website.

4. When information may be shared

We may share information with providers that help host the website, communicate with customers, process an agreed payment, maintain records, prevent fraud or provide professional advice. Providers should receive only information reasonably needed for their role.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, in connection with a business transfer, or with your direction or consent. We do not state that information is “sold” or “shared” for targeted advertising because the current site does not use an advertising or data-sale program.

5. Retention and security

We retain inquiry and transaction information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including customer service, accounting, legal and dispute-resolution needs. Retention periods vary by record type.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not send payment card numbers or other highly sensitive information by ordinary email or social-media message.

6. Your choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct or delete information you previously provided, subject to legal exceptions and recordkeeping obligations. You may also ask questions about how your information is handled. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You can control third-party cookies and tracking through the settings and privacy controls provided by your browser and the relevant third-party service.

7. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through the website. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and address it.

8. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes should be reviewed before new data practices begin.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to Avanti Jewels at avantijewelsny@gmail.com or +1 (718) 697-9678.
