Accessibility information / October 5, 2026

Using the website

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. A skip link takes you to the main content. Search and filters have labels; product links include their names and numbers. Availability is written as text, not conveyed only by color. Browser zoom and reduced-motion settings are supported.

Image viewer controls

Open a product image with its button. Escape closes the viewer. Left/Right arrows change images; plus/minus adjust zoom; 0 resets it. Visible buttons provide the same actions. Tab stays within the open viewer and focus returns when it closes.

If something is difficult to use

We aim to improve accessibility but do not claim that every page or third-party service is independently certified. Tell us the page or product number, the problem and your preferred contact method.
